PROVING IDENTITIES

Rewrite until both sides agree.

An identity is true for every allowed value of the angle. Your job is to turn one side into the other, one legal step at a time.

Before you start

  • The domain means the allowed angles. In every proof below, all original denominators must be nonzero. Tangent also requires cosine to be nonzero.
  • means square the sine value, not the angle. The same convention applies to cosine and higher powers.
  • The left-hand side (LHS) is left of the equality sign; the right-hand side (RHS) is right of it. A factor is one piece of a product. Cancel only common nonzero factors, never individual terms in a sum.
  • To expand brackets, multiply each term in one bracket by each term in the other. To factor, rewrite a sum as a product. A common denominator lets us add fractions because their pieces then have the same size.
