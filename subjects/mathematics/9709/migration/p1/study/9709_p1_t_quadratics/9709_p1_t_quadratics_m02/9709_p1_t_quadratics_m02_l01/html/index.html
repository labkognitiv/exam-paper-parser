Completing the square

Keep the outside number
through every step.

Some expressions begin with 2 times a number times itself, or 3 times a number times itself. The familiar method still works. One extra multiplication keeps every step equal.

Three ideas before the new step

  • The letter xxx represents a number that can vary.
  • A quadratic is an expression whose highest power of xxx is 2. The power 2 means x×xx\times xx×x.
  • In ax2+bx+cax^2+bx+cax2+bx+c, aaa, bbb and ccc are coefficients: fixed numbers multiplying a term or standing alone.
  • A quadratic is monic when a=1a=1a=1. Here we extend completing the square to a≠1a\ne1a=1.

What the leading coefficient changes

  • A parabola is the U-shaped or upside-down U-shaped graph of a quadratic.
  • The vertex is its turning point: the point where the graph changes direction.
  • A point (u,v)(u,v)(u,v) records its horizontal coordinate first and its vertical coordinate second.
  • In a(x+h)2+ka(x+h)^2+ka(x+h)2+k, the bracket locates the vertex at (−h,k)(-h,k)(−h,k).
  • If a>0a>0a>0, the graph opens upward and kkk is its minimum, or lowest, value.
  • If a<0a<0a<0, it opens downward and kkk is its maximum, or highest, value.
  • The notation ∣a∣|a|∣a∣ means the magnitude of aaa: its distance from zero without a negative sign.
  • Larger ∣a∣|a|∣a∣ makes the parabola narrower; smaller non-zero ∣a∣|a|∣a∣ makes it wider.

The sign decides minimum or maximum. The bracket still decides the position.

The six-step method

  1. Start with ax2+bx+cax^2+bx+cax2+bx+c.
  2. Factor aaa from the first two terms: a(x2+bax)+ca(x^2+\frac ba x)+ca(x2+ab​x)+c.
  3. Inside the bracket, halve the coefficient of xxx. Call the result hhh, so h=b2ah=\frac{b}{2a}h=2ab​.
  4. Add and subtract h2h^2h2 inside the bracket.
  5. Turn the first three terms into (x+h)2(x+h)^2(x+h)2. When −h2-h^2−h2 leaves the bracket, it is multiplied by aaa.
  6. Simplify the constant to obtain a(x+h)2+(c−ah2)a(x+h)^2+(c-ah^2)a(x+h)2+(c−ah2).

Worked example

  1. Read the coefficients: a=2, b=8, c=3a=2,\ b=8,\ c=3a=2, b=8, c=3.
  2. Factor 2 from the first two terms: 2(x2+4x)+32(x^2+4x)+32(x2+4x)+3.
