Disguised quadratics

Reveal the quadratic underneath.

Fractions, repeated powers and square roots can hide a familiar quadratic. Clear or rename the repeated structure, solve, then check.

Start the lesson · Go to practice

Rational equations

  • A denominator is the expression below a fraction bar.
  • A rational equation contains fractions of polynomials. This lesson focuses on equations with the unknown in a denominator, such as 2x−1+3=x\frac2{x-1}+3=xx−12​+3=x.
  • A denominator can never equal zero. Write these restrictions before multiplying.
  • A common denominator is an expression divisible by every denominator. Multiplying by it clears all fractions.

Spot the rational equation

  • x+1x=x−3x+2\frac{x+1}{x}=\frac{x-3}{x+2}xx+1​=x+2x−3​ is fraction = fraction.
  • In 3x+4x+2\frac3x+\frac4{x+2}x3​+x+24​, the restrictions are x≠0,−2x\ne0,-2x=0,−2.

Five steps

  1. List every denominator and its forbidden x-values.
  2. Multiply every term on both sides by the lowest common denominator.
  3. Rearrange to ax2+bx+c=0ax^2+bx+c=0ax2+bx+c=0.
  4. Solve the quadratic.
  5. Reject any solution that is forbidden.

Solve 2x−1+3=x\frac2{x-1}+3=xx−12​+3=x.

Solution

  2. Multiply every term by x−1x-1x−1: 2+3(x−1)=x(x−1)2+3(x-1)=x(x-1)2+3(x−1)=x(x−1).

  4. Complete the square: (x−2)2=3(x-2)^2=3(x−2)2=3.
  5. x=2±3x=2\pm\sqrt3x=2±3​. Neither value is 1.

x=2+3x=2+\sqrt3x=2+3​ or x=2−3x=2-\sqrt3x=2−3​.

Key ideas

  • For a rational equation, list forbidden values first.
  • Multiply every term on both sides by the lowest common denominator.
  • Check final roots against the restrictions.
  • For repeated powers or roots, substitute for the expression whose square also appears.
  • Solve in uuu, then back-substitute to find xxx.
  • If x2=ax^2=ax2=a with a>0a>0a>0, use x=±ax=\pm\sqrt ax=±a​.
  • If x3=ax^3=ax3=a, take the one real cube root.
  • If u=xu=\sqrt{x}u=x​ or an even power, keep u≥0u\ge0u≥0.

More rational examples

Single fraction

Solve x+4x−1=5x+\frac4{x-1}=5x+x−14​=5.

Solution

  1. x≠1x\ne1x=1.
  2. Multiply every term by x−1x-1x−1: x(x−1)+4=5(x−1)x(x-1)+4=5(x-1)x(x−1)+4=5(x−1).

Two fractions

Solve 3x+4x+1=2\frac3x+\frac4{x+1}=2x3​+x+14​=2.
