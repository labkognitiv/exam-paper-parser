Completing the square

Make the turning point visible.

A quadratic can hide its lowest point. Rewriting it as one squared bracket plus a constant reveals the graph at once.

Start the lesson · Go to practice

One quadratic, two forms

  • The letter xxx is a variable: it can take different number values.
  • A quadratic is an expression whose highest power of the variable is 2, with a nonzero coefficient of x2x^2x2.
  • The exponent 2 means multiply xxx by itself.
  • A coefficient is the number multiplying a term. In x2+6x+5x^2+6x+5x2+6x+5, the coefficient of xxx is 6.
  • Monic means the coefficient of x2x^2x2 is 1.
  • Expanding removes brackets by multiplying every term.

Expand a squared bracket

(x+3)2=(x+3)(x+3)=x2+6x+9(x+3)^2=(x+3)(x+3)=x^2+6x+9(x+3)2=(x+3)(x+3)=x2+6x+9

The two middle products are 3x+3x=6x3x+3x=6x3x+3x=6x.

  • Square form, also called completed-square form, reverses this expansion.
  • The expressions below are equal for every value of xxx.
  • For each chosen xxx, the equation gives an output called yyy.
  • The graph of a quadratic equation is a U-shaped curve called a parabola.
  • A coordinate pair (x,y)(x,y)(x,y) gives a point: first its horizontal x-position, then its vertical y-position.
y=x2+6x+5=(x+3)2−4y=x^2+6x+5=(x+3)^2-4y=x2+6x+5=(x+3)2−4

The square form tells you where the curve turns.

Remember both signs

  • Squaring a positive or negative number can give the same result.
  • 42=164^2=1642=16 and (−4)2=16(-4)^2=16(−4)2=16.
  • So the equation x2=16x^2=16x2=16 has two solutions: x=4x=4x=4 or x=−4x=-4x=−4.
  • The square-root symbol means the non-negative square root: 16=4\sqrt{16}=416​=4. Solving a squared equation requires checking both signs.

The turning point

The turning point is the lowest or highest point of a parabola. Its coordinates are written (x,y)(x,y)(x,y).

For a monic quadratic

y=(x+h)2+ky=(x+h)^2+ky=(x+h)2+k
  • The square is smallest at zero, so the bracket must equal zero.
  • x+h=0x+h=0x+h=0, so x=−hx=-hx=−h.
  • The sign of the turning point's x-coordinate is opposite to the sign inside the bracket.
  • At that x-value, the square is 0, so y=ky=ky=k.
Turning point (−h,k)(-h,k)(−h,k)
Axis of symmetry x=−hx=-hx=−h
Minimum value y=ky=ky=k
Range y≥ky\ge ky≥k
  • The axis of symmetry is the vertical line through the middle of the parabola.
  • The minimum value is the smallest y-value reached.
  • The range is the set of all possible output values.
  • These minimum and range facts apply because the coefficient of the square is positive, so the graph opens upwards.

Read the facts from y=(x+3)2−4y=(x+3)^2-4y=(x+3)2−4.

  1. x+3=0x+3=0x+3=0, so x=−3x=-3x=−3.
  2. The turning point is (−3,−4)(-3,-4)(−3,−4) .
  3. The axis of symmetry is x=−3x=-3x=−3 .
  4. The minimum value is −4-4−4 .
  5. The range is y≥−4y\ge-4y≥−4 .

Why halving works

  • Use a letter hhh for the number inside the bracket.
  • Expand the bracket and collect the two middle terms.
(x+h)2=(x+h)(x+h)=x2+2hx+h2(x+h)^2=(x+h)(x+h)=x^2+2hx+h^2(x+h)2=(x+h)(x+h)=x2+2hx+h2
  • The coefficient of xxx is 2h2h2h.
  • Therefore hhh is half the original x-coefficient.
  • Building the square introduces h2h^2h2.
  • Add and subtract that same value. The total change is zero, so equality is preserved.

The general method

x2+px+q=x2+px+h2−h2+q=(x+h)2+(q−h2),h=p2x^2+px+q=x^2+px+h^2-h^2+q=(x+h)^2+(q-h^2),\quad h=\frac p2x2+px+q=x2+px+h2−h2+q=(x+h)2+(q−h2),h=2p​
  1. Start with x2+px+qx^2+px+qx2+px+q.
  2. Identify the signed coefficient ppp.
  3. Halve ppp to get hhh.
  4. Square hhh to get h2h^2h2.
  5. Add and subtract h2h^2h2.
  6. Group the first three terms.
  7. Rewrite them as (x+h)2(x+h)^2(x+h)2.
  8. Simplify the constants.

Worked example

Rewrite x2+6x+5x^2+6x+5x2+6x+5 in completed-square form.

Solution

  1. The x-coefficient is p=6p=6p=6.
  2. Half of 6 is 3.
  3. The square of 3 is 9.
  4. x2+6x+5=x2+6x+9−9+5x^2+6x+5=x^2+6x+9-9+5x2+6x+5=x2+6x+9−9+5
  5. =(x2+6x+9)−9+5=(x^2+6x+9)-9+5=(x2+6x+9)−9+5
  6. =(x+3)2−9+5=(x+3)^2-9+5=(x+3)2−9+5
  7. =(x+3)2−4=(x+3)^2-4=(x+3)2−4

Watch out

Turning point sign

Wrong: y=(x+3)2−4y=(x+3)^2-4y=(x+3)2−4 has turning point (3,−4)(3,-4)(3,−4).

Correct: set x+3=0x+3=0x+3=0, so the turning point is (−3,−4)(-3,-4)(−3,−4).

Compensating for the added square

Wrong: x2+8x+5=(x+4)2+5x^2+8x+5=(x+4)^2+5x2+8x+5=(x+4)2+5.

Correct: the bracket introduces 16, so subtract 16: x2+8x+5=(x+4)2−11x^2+8x+5=(x+4)^2-11x2+8x+5=(x+4)2−11.

Keep the sign when halving

Wrong: half the 6 in x2−6x+2x^2-6x+2x2−6x+2.

Correct: the signed coefficient is -6. Half is -3, so x2−6x+2=(x−3)2−7x^2-6x+2=(x-3)^2-7x2−6x+2=(x−3)2−7.
