Forces and equilibrium · Module 2 · Lesson 1

Unknown force and angle

When three forces balance, the horizontal parts cancel and the vertical parts cancel. Those two equations rebuild an unknown force and its direction.

Key ideasWorked examplesRecap

Equilibrium gives two equations

  • A particle is modelled as a single point.
  • Coplanar forces act in one flat plane.
  • Equilibrium means there is no resultant force.
  • The x-axis is horizontal. The y-axis is vertical.
  • Choose right and up as positive directions.
  • Then total right equals total left, and total up equals total down.
  • The symbol ∑\sum∑ is the Greek capital letter sigma. Here it means “add all the force components”.
  • In symbols: ∑Fx=0\sum F_x=0∑Fx​=0 and ∑Fy=0\sum F_y=0∑Fy​=0.
  • N\text{N}N means newton, the unit of force.

Resolve one angled force

  • FFF is the force magnitude. θ\thetaθ is its angle from the positive x-axis.
  • The force and its perpendicular components form a right-angled triangle. A right angle is exactly 90°.
  • The force is the hypotenuse, the longest side opposite the right angle.
  • The horizontal side is adjacent to θ\thetaθ: Fcos⁡θF\cos\thetaFcosθ.
  • The vertical side is opposite θ\thetaθ: Fsin⁡θF\sin\thetaFsinθ.
  • The diagram decides each sign.

The five-step method

  1. Choose x and y axes and positive directions.
  2. Draw and label each force component.
  3. Write the horizontal balance equation.
  4. Write the vertical balance equation.
  5. Use Pythagoras and inverse tangent to recover the force and angle.

Rebuild a force from components

If X=Fcos⁡θX=F\cos\thetaX=Fcosθ and Y=Fsin⁡θY=F\sin\thetaY=Fsinθ, then

F=X2+Y2F=\sqrt{X^2+Y^2}F=X2+Y2​
θ=tan⁡−1 ⁣(YX)\theta=\tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{Y}{X}\right)θ=tan−1(XY​)
  • X2X^2X2 means X×XX\times XX×X.
  • The positive square root gives the force magnitude.
  • tan⁡−1\tan^{-1}tan−1 means inverse tangent: it turns a component ratio into an angle.
  • A quadrant is one of the four regions made by the x-axis and y-axis.
  • Use the signs of XXX and YYY to choose the correct quadrant.

Worked examples

Worked example

First check whether the directions can balance

Consider these three forces, stated to be in equilibrium:

  • FFF acts 60° above the positive x-axis.
  • 2F2F2F acts horizontally right.
  • 15 N acts up and left, at angle α\alphaα above the negative x-axis.
Solution
  1. Inspect the vertical directions before calculating.
  2. The force FFF has upward component Fsin⁡60∘F\sin60^\circFsin60∘.
  3. The 15 N force has upward component 15sin⁡α15\sin\alpha15sinα.
  4. The force 2F2F2F has no vertical component.
  5. Fsin⁡60∘+15sin⁡α=0F\sin60^\circ+15\sin\alpha=0Fsin60∘+15sinα=0
  6. For positive FFF and an acute α\alphaα, both terms are positive. Their sum cannot be zero.

The stated directions cannot produce equilibrium.

If the 15 N force points down-left

If the 15 N force acts down and left at angle α\alphaα below the negative x-axis, its vertical component can oppose FFF.

  1. Horizontal balance:
  2. 2F+Fcos⁡60∘=15cos⁡α2F+F\cos60^\circ=15\cos\alpha2F+Fcos60∘=15cosα
  3. 2.5F=15cos⁡α2.5F=15\cos\alpha2.5F=15cosα
  4. Vertical balance:
  5. Fsin⁡60∘=15sin⁡αF\sin60^\circ=15\sin\alphaFsin60∘=15sinα
  6. Divide the vertical equation by the horizontal equation. The common factors cancel:
  7. tan⁡α=Fsin⁡60∘2.5F=0.346410…\tan\alpha=\frac{F\sin60^\circ}{2.5F}=0.346410\ldotstanα=2.5FFsin60∘​=0.346410…
  8. α=19.1066…∘\alpha=19.1066\ldots^\circα=19.1066…∘
  9. Substitute into either component equation:
  10. F=15sin⁡19.1066…∘sin⁡60∘=5.66947… NF=\frac{15\sin19.1066\ldots^\circ}{\sin60^\circ}=5.66947\ldots\text{ N}F=sin60∘15sin19.1066…∘​=5.66947… N

Answer for this direction: F=5.67 NF=5.67\text{ N}F=5.67 N, α=19.1∘\alpha=19.1^\circα=19.1∘.

Worked example

Use exact triangle ratios

Three forces act at a point:

  • 78 N acts down and right, making angle α\alphaα below the positive x-axis.
  • 50 N acts down and left, making angle θ\thetaθ below the negative x-axis.
  • 112 N acts vertically upward.
  • If tan⁡α=12/5\tan\alpha=12/5tanα=12/5, draw a right triangle with adjacent side 5 and opposite side 12.
  • Pythagoras gives the hypotenuse 52+122=13\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=1352+122​=13.
  • So sin⁡α=12/13\sin\alpha=12/13sinα=12/13 and cos⁡α=5/13\cos\alpha=5/13cosα=5/13.
  • If tan⁡θ=4/3\tan\theta=4/3tanθ=4/3, the 3-4-5 triangle gives sin⁡θ=4/5\sin\theta=4/5sinθ=4/5 and cos⁡θ=3/5\cos\theta=3/5cosθ=3/5.
Equilibrium check
  1. Horizontal components:
  2. 78(513)−50(35)=30−30=078\left(\frac5{13}\right)-50\left(\frac35\right)=30-30=078(135​)−50(53​)=30−30=0
  3. Vertical components:
  4. 112−78(1213)−50(45)=112−72−40=0112-78\left(\frac{12}{13}\right)-50\left(\frac45\right)=112-72-40=0112−78(1312​)−50(54​)=112−72−40=0

Both component totals are zero, so the forces balance.

Measure the angle from the correct axis

A 10 N force makes an angle of 30° with the vertical. The vertical component lies beside the angle, so it uses cosine. The horizontal component lies opposite the angle, so it uses sine.

Key ideas

  • Check whether the shown directions can cancel before solving.
  • Write one horizontal and one vertical balance equation.
  • Choose sine or cosine from the side beside or opposite the stated angle.
  • Use exact triangle ratios where available.
  • Rebuild magnitude with Pythagoras and direction with inverse tangent.
  • Use component signs to choose the quadrant.

Exam-style direction check

Three forces are stated to be in equilibrium:

  • 20 N acts 40° above the negative x-direction, so its direction from +x is 140°.
  • 100 N acts 20° to the right of downward vertical, so its direction from +x is -70°.
  • An unknown force FFF completes equilibrium.

The unknown must be the negative of the combined known-force components.

Solution
  1. Resolve the two known forces:
  2. Kx=20cos⁡140∘+100cos⁡(−70∘)=18.8811…K_x=20\cos140^\circ+100\cos(-70^\circ)=18.8811\ldotsKx​=20cos140∘+100cos(−70∘)=18.8811…

  4. For equilibrium, the unknown force reverses both totals:
  5. Fx=−18.8811…,Fy=81.1135…F_x=-18.8811\ldots,\qquad F_y=81.1135\ldotsFx​=−18.8811…,Fy​=81.1135…
  6. F=Fx2+Fy2=83.2820… NF=\sqrt{F_x^2+F_y^2}=83.2820\ldots\text{ N}F=Fx2​+Fy2​​=83.2820… N
  7. The components are left and up, so FFF lies in the second quadrant.
  8. First find the acute reference angle β\betaβ from the positive component sizes:
  9. β=tan⁡−1 ⁣(81.1135…18.8811…)=76.8964…∘\beta=\tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{81.1135\ldots}{18.8811\ldots}\right)=76.8964\ldots^\circβ=tan−1(18.8811…81.1135…​)=76.8964…∘
