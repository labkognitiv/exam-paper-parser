Momentum is mass multiplied by signed velocity. A system is the group being considered. An external impulse is a push from outside that group which changes its total momentum.

Key ideas

  • Choose one positive direction and keep every velocity signed.
  • Use one momentum equation per isolated collision.
  • Carry each answer into the next before-state.
  • At a wall, use the stated rebound rule; the wall supplies external impulse.
  • Use position and relative velocity to test whether another collision happens.
  • Total kinetic-energy change measures collision loss only when no work or energy transfer occurs between the chosen start and end states.
