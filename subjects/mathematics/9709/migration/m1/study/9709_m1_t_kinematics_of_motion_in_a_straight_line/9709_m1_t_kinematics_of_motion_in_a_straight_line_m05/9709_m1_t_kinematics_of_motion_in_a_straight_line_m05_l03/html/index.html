Total distance and sign-aware integration

Use a velocity function to separate signed displacement from total distance travelled.

Start the lesson
SetupWorked examplesKey ideas

Before integrating

  • Velocity v is signed: positive means forward and negative means backward.
  • Displacement is signed change in position. Distance is the total positive path length.
  • For powers, ∫tⁿdt=tⁿ⁺¹/(n+1) when n≠−1. Integrating v(t) over a time interval adds signed displacement. The integral needs limits, such as 0≤t≤8. Average speed is total distance divided by total time: 3790 m ÷ 400 s = 9.475 m s⁻¹, which rounds to 9.48 m s⁻¹.
  • At a turning point, velocity is zero and changes sign. Solve v(t)=0 first, then split the integral at each turning time.
  • For each interval, take the magnitude of its displacement before adding for total distance. The symbols |x| mean the non-negative magnitude of x; Δs means one interval change; Σ means add all listed intervals.

Position from velocity

s=∫v(t)dt    distance=Σ|Δs|

Time is measured in seconds, position and distance in metres, velocity in m s⁻¹.

Worked examples

Worked example

Split at turning points

v=t²−8t+12 for 0≤t≤8. Find total distance.

  1. Set v=0: t²−8t+12=(t−2)(t−6)=0, so turning times are 2 s and 6 s.
  2. Integrate: s=∫vdt=t³/3−4t²+12t.
  3. Displacements: s(2)−s(0)=32/3; s(6)−s(2)=−32/3; s(8)−s(6)=32/3.
  4. Add magnitudes: 32/3+32/3+32/3=32m.

Total distance = 32 m.

Worked example

Distance until first rest

v=6t−0.3t². Find distance from O until first rest.

  1. 0=6t−0.3t²=t(6−0.3t), so first rest after projection is t=20s.
  2. s=∫(6t−0.3t²)dt=3t²−0.1t³.
  3. s(20)=1200−800=400m.

OX = 400 m.

Worked example

Between two rest times

v=4t²−8t+3. Find distance between the two rest times.

  1. 0=4t²−8t+3=(2t−1)(2t−3), so t=0.5,1.5.
  2. s=∫vdt=4t³/3−4t²+3t.
  3. s(1.5)=0 and s(0.5)=2/3. Distance is |0−2/3|=2/3m.

Distance = 2/3 m.

Worked example

Use position values

s=t²−3t for 0≤t≤6. Find distance when rest occurs at t=1.5.

  1. s(0)=0, s(1.5)=−2.25, s(6)=18.
  2. Distance 0 to 1.5: |−2.25−0|=2.25. Distance 1.5 to 6: |18−(−2.25)|=20.25.
  3. 2.25+20.25=22.5m.

Total distance = 22.5 m.

Key ideas

  • Average speed = total distance ÷ total time. Average velocity = signed displacement ÷ total time.
  • Average velocity = signed displacement ÷ total time.
  • A single integral can cancel forward and backward travel, so it may give displacement only.
  • Split at every solution of v=0.

A sign change is the signal to split.

Worked example

Try it yourself

v=2t²−6t+4 for 0≤t≤4.

  1. Rest times: 2(t−1)(t−2)=0, so t=1,2.
  2. s=2t³/3−3t²+4t; values are s(0)=0,s(1)=5/3,s(2)=4/3,s(4)=32/3.
  3. Distance: |5/3|+|4/3−5/3|+|32/3−4/3|=34/3≈11.3m.

Total distance = 34/3 m ≈ 11.3 m.

Worked example

Exam-style practice

v=t²−5t+4 for 0≤t≤6. Find rest times and total distance.

  1. v=0⇒(t−1)(t−4)=0, so rest at 1 s and 4 s.
  2. s=t³/3−5t²/2+4t; s(0)=0,s(1)=11/6,s(4)=−8/3,s(6)=6.
  3. distance=|11/6|+|−8/3−11/6|+|6−(−8/3)|=15m.

Rest times 1 s and 4 s; total distance = 15 m.
