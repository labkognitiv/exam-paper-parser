Mechanics 1 · Module 6 · Lesson 1

Build v(t) and s(t) by integrating

Integration moves down the motion ladder: acceleration to velocity to displacement.

StartWorked examplesChecksRecapMore examples

The integration ladder

  • a(t) is acceleration in m/s². Integrating it gives v(t), velocity in m/s.
  • Integrating v(t) gives s(t), displacement in metres.
  • For every indefinite integral, write a new constant: +C for velocity and +D for displacement.
  • Power rule: ∫tndt=tn+1/(n+1)+C. This rule requires n ≠ −1. Add 1 to the power, then divide by the new power. The constant C is needed because differentiating any constant gives zero.
  • A condition such as v(0)=5 or s(3)=12 fixes the relevant constant.
Six steps
  1. Start with the given function.
  2. Integrate once to move from a to v, or again to s.
  3. Write +C or +D.
  4. Substitute the given time and value.
  5. Solve the constant line by line.
  6. Rewrite the final expression with units understood.

Worked examples

Acceleration to velocity

a=4t−6 and v=5 when t=0.

  1. v=∫(4t−6)dt=2t²−6t+C.
  2. 5=2(0)²−6(0)+C, so C=5.
  3. v=2t²−6t+5.

Velocity to displacement

v=3t²−8t+4 and the particle starts from O, so s(0)=0.

  1. s=∫(3t²−8t+4)dt=t³−4t²+4t+C.
  2. 0=C at t=0.
  3. s=t³−4t²+4t.

Two integrations

a=0.6t²−2; starts from rest at O.

  1. v=∫(0.6t²−2)dt=0.2t³−2t+C.
  2. v(0)=0 gives C=0.
  3. s=∫(0.2t³−2t)dt=0.05t⁴−t²+D.
  4. s(0)=0 gives D=0.

s=0.05t⁴−t².

Condition away from zero

v=5t−3 and s=12 when t=2.

  1. s=∫(5t−3)dt=2.5t²−3t+C.
  2. 12=2.5(2)²−3(2)+C=10−6+C.
  3. C=8, so s=2.5t²−3t+8.

Two displacement conditions

a=6t−2, with s(1)=7 and s(3)=29.

  1. v=∫(6t−2)dt=3t²−2t+C.
  2. s=∫(3t²−2t+C)dt=t³−t²+Ct+D.
  3. s(1)=7 gives C+D=7.
  4. s(3)=29 gives 3C+D=11.
  5. Subtract: 2C=4, so C=2; then D=5.

s=t³−t²+2t+5.

Try it yourself

a=3t²−4 and v=6 when t=1.

  1. v=∫(3t²−4)dt=t³−4t+C.
  2. 6=1−4+C, so C=9.

v=t³−4t+9.

Keep the constant

v=4t−1 and s=10 when t=2.

  1. s=∫(4t−1)dt=2t²−t+C.
  2. 10=2(2)²−2+C=6+C, so C=4.

s=2t²−t+4.

Exam-style

a=8t−6, v=2 and s=5 at t=0.

  1. v=∫(8t−6)dt=4t²−6t+C; v(0)=2 gives C=2.
  2. s=∫(4t²−6t+2)dt=4/3t³−3t²+2t+D; s(0)=5 gives D=5.

s=4/3t³−3t²+2t+5.

Recap

  • Integrate a to obtain v, then v to obtain s.
  • Write a fresh constant after each indefinite integration.
  • Use the condition matching the function to fix its constant.
  • Check powers, signs, units and the stated time/domain.

More examples

Choose a new condition such as v(2) or s(3), integrate first, then solve its constant.
