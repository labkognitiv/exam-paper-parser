Journey shapes on a v-t graph

Accelerate, cruise, then decelerate. The velocity-time graph is a trapezium.

StartWorked examplesChecksRecapMore examples

Read the three intervals

  • Time t is measured in seconds on the horizontal axis. Velocity v is measured in metres per second vertically.
  • The left straight slope is positive acceleration. The flat top is constant speed. The right slope is negative acceleration, or deceleration.
  • A trapezium has two parallel sides. Its area is ½(a+b)h. Here the parallel sides are the time lengths and the height is greatest speed V.
  • Area under a v-t graph gives displacement. When velocity stays non-negative, that displacement is total distance.
  • Use V=u+at for a straight acceleration section. Let τa, τc and τd be the three stage durations; cumulative join times are t₁=τa and t₂=τa+τc, with final time t₃=τa+τc+τd. Then τa=V/a and τd=V/d.
Area breakdowndistance=½t1V+(t2−t1)V+½(t3−t2)V

Worked examples

Times from rates

Greatest speed is V. Acceleration is a; deceleration magnitude is d; total time is T.

  1. From rest, V=0+a t1, so t1=V/a.
  2. To rest, 0=V−d t3, so t3=V/d.
  3. Constant-speed time is t2=T−t1−t3.

Train journey

A train accelerates at 0.025 m/s² for 600 s, travels at 15 m/s for 2600 s, then decelerates at 0.0375 m/s² to rest.

  1. V=at=0.025(600)=15 m/s.
  2. t3=V/d=15/0.0375=400 s.
  3. T=600+2600+400=3600 s.

Greatest speed 15 m/s; deceleration time 400 s; total time 3600 s.

Distance from the trapezium

Use greatest speed 15 m/s, constant-speed time 2600 s and total time 3600 s. The intervals are 600 s, 2600 s, and 400 s, so the right interval is 3200 to 3600 s.

  1. Trapezium: ½(2600+3600)(15)=46,500 m.
  2. Check by pieces: ½(600)(15)=4500, 2600(15)=39000, ½(400)(15)=3000.
  3. 4500+39000+3000=46500 m.

Total distance = 46,500 m.

Find peak speed

A car accelerates at 0.3 m/s², decelerates at 1 m/s², total time 552 s and distance 1340 m. Let peak speed be V.

  1. t1=V/0.3; t3=V/1=V; t2=552−V/0.3−V.
  2. Area equation: 1340=½(V/0.3)V+(552−V/0.3−V)V+½V².
  3. Collect terms: 0=−13/6 V²+552V−1340.
  4. Quadratic roots are V≈252.318112 or V≈2.451118. The first makes constant-speed time negative, so reject it.

Peak speed = 2.45 m/s.

From distances to time

A cyclist accelerates from rest at 0.5 m/s² for 36 m, travels 150 m at constant speed, then slows for 24 m to rest.

  1. 36=½(0.5)t1², so t1²=144 and t1=12 s. Check: V²=2(0.5)(36)=36, so V=6 m/s.
  2. 150=6t2, so t2=25 s.
  3. 24=½(6+0)t3, so t3=8 s.
  4. T=12+25+8=45 s.

Total time = 45 s.

Displacement-time shape

For a positive trapezium v-t journey, the s-t graph has three pieces: a curve getting steeper while velocity increases, a straight line while speed is constant, then a curve getting less steep while velocity decreases. Its gradient is velocity.

Lift self-check

A lift accelerates from rest at 0.9 m/s² for 3 s, travels at constant speed for 6 s, then decelerates to rest in 4 s.

  1. V=0+0.9(3)=2.7 m/s.
  2. distance=½(6+13)(2.7)=25.65 m, because the trapezium's parallel time sides are 6 s and 13 s.

Greatest speed = 2.7 m/s; total distance = 25.65 m.

Exam-style journey

A car accelerates from rest at 2 m/s² for 10 s, travels at constant speed for 30 s, then decelerates to rest in 20 s.

  1. V=2(10)=20 m/s.
  2. A1=½(10)(20)=100 m, A2=30(20)=600 m, A3=½(20)(20)=200 m.
  3. distance=100+600+200=900 m.

Greatest speed = 20 m/s; total distance = 900 m.

Recap

  • Label accelerating, constant-speed and decelerating intervals with their original names.
  • Use slopes for acceleration and trapezium/triangle/rectangle areas for distance.
  • Use positive deceleration magnitude when finding stopping time.
  • Check every time is non-negative and every area uses seconds times metres per second.

More examples

Exact train graph

The train graph has joins at t=600, 3200 and 3600 s, with top speed 15 m/s. Its areas are 4500 m, 39000 m and 3000 m, totalling 46500 m.

Lift journey

A lift accelerates from rest at 0.9 m/s² for 3 s, cruises for 6 s, then decelerates to rest for 4 s.

  1. V=0+0.9(3)=2.7 m/s.
  2. The parallel time sides are 6 s and 13 s: distance=½(6+13)(2.7)=25.65 m.

Car journey

A car accelerates at 2 m/s² for 10 s, cruises for 30 s, then decelerates for 20 s.

  1. V=2(10)=20 m/s.
  2. distance=½(10)(20)+30(20)+½(20)(20)=900 m.
