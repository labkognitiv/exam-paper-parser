Centre and spread

Order the data.
Find its middle.

The median marks the centre. Quartiles show how the ordered values divide, and the interquartile range measures the spread of the middle half.

Start the lesson · Key ideas

Order raw data first

  • Raw data are the values as they were collected.
  • Ascending order means smallest to largest.
  • Ordering makes repeated values and middle positions easy to see.
  • The mode is the value that occurs most often.

The median is the middle

  • The median is the middle of an ordered data set.
  • An odd number of values leaves one value in the middle.
  • An even number of values leaves two middle values.
  • For two middle values, add them and divide by 2. This gives their average.

Why median and IQR resist extremes

  • An extreme value lies far from most of the data.
  • A measure is resistant if one extreme change has little effect on it.
  • The median and quartiles depend on middle positions, so changing only an end value may leave them unchanged.
