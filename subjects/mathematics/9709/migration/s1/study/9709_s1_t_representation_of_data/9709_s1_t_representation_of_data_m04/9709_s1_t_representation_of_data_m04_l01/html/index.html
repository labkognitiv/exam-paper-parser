Combine data sets through totals

Do not average two means or two standard deviations. First recover each group's count, total and squared total.

In this section, let denote a value in the first group and a value in the second group. Let and be their group counts. Let denote any value after the groups have joined.

A larger group contributes more values, so it has more weight in the combined mean. Combined spread includes variation within each group and the separation between their means.
