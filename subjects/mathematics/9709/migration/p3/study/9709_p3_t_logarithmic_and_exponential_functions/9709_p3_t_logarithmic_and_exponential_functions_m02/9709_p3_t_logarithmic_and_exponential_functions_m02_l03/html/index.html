Key ideas

  • Split a rational expression into partial fractions first.
  • Rewrite each denominator so the powered bracket begins with 1.
  • Expand each fraction and multiply every numerator term.
  • Collect coefficients of equal powers.
  • Find the validity condition for every non-terminating series.
  • Use the intersection of those conditions.
  • When a question says “hence”, use the earlier result.
