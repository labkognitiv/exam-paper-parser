Match the method to the question

The interval includes both endpoints. The open interval excludes them.

  • To verify a root in an interval, move everything to one side and evaluate both endpoints.
  • To show exactly one root, compare suitable curves and justify one crossing.
  • If an iteration is stated to converge, replace both consecutive terms by its limit.
  • To find an exact limit after iteration, solve the limiting equation exactly.
