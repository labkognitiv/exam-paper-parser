# Cambridge Physics (9702) Question Typology & Testing Pattern Reference

## Executive Overview

This reference deconstructs Cambridge examination questioning methods across the complete 2016-2025 past paper archive (8335 mapped items across Paper 1 MCQ, Paper 2 AS Theory, and Paper 4 A2 Theory).
Using syntactic positional n-grams and mark scheme phrase mining, it identifies the six foundational question archetypes, authentic prompt templates, and examiner reward criteria.
