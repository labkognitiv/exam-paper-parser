# Common AS Physics P1 Skill Mappings

Controlled AS curriculum topics (9702_t01 to 9702_t11) and their registered, compatible skills:

## Topic 1: Physical quantities and units (`9702_t01`)
- `9702_skill_base_derived_units`: Base SI units, derived units, dimensional homogeneity
- `9702_skill_si_units_homogeneity`: Checking equations for homogeneity
- `9702_skill_physical_estimations`: Estimating physical orders of magnitude
- `9702_skill_errors_uncertainties`: Absolute, fractional, and percentage uncertainties
- `9702_skill_scalars_vectors`: Distinguishing scalars and vectors
- `9702_skill_add_vectors`: Vector addition and resolution

## Topic 2: Kinematics (`9702_t02`)
- `9702_skill_displacement_velocity_acceleration`: Definitions and basic kinematics relations
- `9702_skill_motion_graphs`: Interpreting and constructing displacement-time and velocity-time graphs
- `9702_skill_kinematics_equations`: Solving constant acceleration problems with suvat equations
- `9702_skill_two_dimensional_motion`: Projectile motion and 2D kinematics

## Topic 3: Dynamics (`9702_t03`)
- `9702_skill_newtons_laws`: Newton's first, second, and third laws of motion
- `9702_skill_linear_momentum_collisions`: Conservation of linear momentum in 1D and 2D
- `9702_skill_impulse_force_time`: Impulse and area under force-time graphs
- `9702_skill_drag_terminal_velocity`: Air resistance, drag forces, and terminal velocity
- `9702_skill_relative_speed_elastic_collision`: Relative velocity in elastic collisions

## Topic 4: Forces, density and pressure (`9702_t04`)
- `9702_skill_moments_couples`: Principle of moments, torque, couples
- `9702_skill_equilibrium_coplanar_forces`: Static equilibrium of coplanar forces (closed vector triangles)
- `9702_skill_density_pressure`: Density (ρ = m/V), pressure (p = F/A), hydrostatic pressure (Δp = ρgΔh)
- `9702_skill_upthrust_archimedes`: Archimedes' principle and upthrust (F_u = ρgV)

## Topic 5: Work, energy and power (`9702_t05`)
- `9702_skill_work_done_general`: Work done (W = Fs cos θ), force-displacement graph area
- `9702_skill_energy_conservation_ke_gpe`: Conservation of energy, kinetic energy (Ek = 1/2 mv²), GPE (Ep = mgh)
- `9702_skill_power_efficiency`: Power (P = W/t = Fv), efficiency (output/input)

## Topic 6: Deformation of solids (`9702_t06`)
- `9702_skill_hookes_law_elastic_energy`: Hooke's law (F = kx), elastic strain energy (Ep = 1/2 Fx = 1/2 kx²), loading/unloading area
- `9702_skill_stress_strain_young_modulus`: Stress (σ = F/A), strain (ε = ΔL/L), Young modulus (E = σ/ε)

## Topic 7: Waves (`9702_t07`)
- `9702_skill_progressive_wave_properties`: Amplitude, wavelength, period, frequency, wave speed (v = fλ)
- `9702_skill_transverse_longitudinal_waves`: Particle displacement and propagation direction
- `9702_skill_wave_intensity_amplitude`: Intensity-amplitude relation (I ∝ A²)
- `9702_skill_doppler_effect`: Observed frequency for moving sound sources (f_o = f_s v / (v ± v_s))
- `9702_skill_em_spectrum_orders`: Electromagnetic spectrum orders of magnitude
- `9702_skill_polarisation`: Malus' law, polarisation of transverse waves

## Topic 8: Superposition (`9702_t08`)
- `9702_skill_stationary_waves`: Node/antinode properties, resonance in strings and pipes
- `9702_skill_two_source_interference`: Double-slit interference (λ = ax/D), phase/path difference
- `9702_skill_diffraction_grating`: Diffraction grating equation (d sin θ = nλ), maximum observable orders
- `9702_skill_single_slit_diffraction`: Single slit diffraction patterns

## Topic 9: Electricity (`9702_t09`)
- `9702_skill_electric_current_drift_speed`: Current as flow of charge carriers (Q = It, I = Anvq)
- `9702_skill_potential_difference_power`: Potential difference (V = W/Q), electrical power (P = VI = I²R = V²/R)
- `9702_skill_resistance_resistivity`: Ohm's law (V = IR), resistivity (R = ρL/A), I-V characteristics

## Topic 10: D.C. circuits (`9702_t10`)
- `9702_skill_emf_internal_resistance`: Electromotive force, internal resistance, terminal p.d. (E = V + Ir)
- `9702_skill_kirchhoffs_laws`: Kirchhoff's first and second laws (ΣI = 0, ΣE = ΣIR)
- `9702_skill_potential_dividers`: Potential divider circuits, potentiometer, LDR/thermistor sensors

## Topic 11: Particle physics (`9702_t11`)
- `9702_skill_nuclear_structure_composition`: Nucleon number A, proton number Z, isotopes
- `9702_skill_nuclear_decay_equations`: Alpha (α), beta-minus (β⁻), beta-plus (β⁺) decay, neutrino emission
- `9702_skill_fundamental_particles_quarks`: Quarks (up, down, strange), hadrons, baryons (qqq), mesons (q q̄), leptons (electrons, neutrinos)
