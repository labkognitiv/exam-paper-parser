speed of 15.0 m s–1.

loudspeaker. The observer hears a sound of varying frequency. The maximum frequency heard
is 2097 Hz.
What is the speed of the sound in the air?
A
294 m s–1
B
309 m s–1
C
324 m s–1

330 m s–1
