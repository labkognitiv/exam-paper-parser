# Physics 9702 enrichment completeness tracker

Last checked: 2026-08-30

The paper tables in this tracker measure enrichment file completeness. Sampled
quality results are recorded separately below and do not certify every question
in a paper.

Status definitions:

- `COMPLETE`: one enrichment record exists for every canonical question in the paper.
- `PARTIAL`: enrichment exists for some, but not all, canonical questions.
- `MISSING`: no enrichment records exist for the paper.

## Closeout rule

A paper moves to `COMPLETE` only when its enrichment count equals its canonical
question count. Accuracy and semantic-review status are tracked separately.
