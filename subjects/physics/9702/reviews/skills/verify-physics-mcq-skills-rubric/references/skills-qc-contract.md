# Skills/rubric QC contract

Exact ledger header:

```text
question_id,primary_skill_fit,supporting_skill_fit,rubric_fit,registry_fit,verdict,notes
```

- Fit fields: `yes` or `no`.
- Verdict: `passed`, `failed`, or `needs_review`.
- Review every question; sampling is forbidden.
- `supporting_skill_fit=yes` means either the assigned second skill is necessary and accurate, or no second skill is correctly required.
- Keep notes blank for straightforward passes; explain failures and uncertainty.
