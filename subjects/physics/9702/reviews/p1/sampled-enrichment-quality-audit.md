# Physics 9702 P1 sampled enrichment quality audit

Last checked: 2026-08-30

## Scope

Q01 was checked from each of the 69 P1 papers against the canonical MCQ and
official answer. This is a sampling audit, not certification of all 40 questions
in a paper.

P1 enrichment does not contain an AI-rubric object. Its checking target is the
`accepted_answer`, followed by instructional `hints` and `walkthrough` text.
The audit therefore checked:

- accepted-answer parity with the canonical question and answer key;
- whether both hints address the actual stem and options;
- whether the walkthrough derives or explains the official answer;
- whether distractor diagnoses address the actual alternatives; and
- deterministic taxonomy, skill, pattern and text-integrity validation.

Uniqueness was not used as a proxy for personalization.

## Results

- Sampled papers: 69
- Sampled questions: 69
- Accepted-answer parity failures: 0
- Deterministic validation failures: 0
- Hint-personalization failures: 0
- Walkthrough accuracy/personalization failures: 0
- Generic distractor-diagnosis reviews: 0
- Sample passes without findings: 69
- Sample reviews: 0
- Sample failures: 0

Every sampled record has at least two progressive hints and at least two
walkthrough steps. The 25 findings from the initial audit were repaired on
2026-08-30 and the full 69-paper deterministic validator passed afterward.

## Repair outcome

The six copied-content failures were rewritten against their canonical stems
and official answers. The nineteen generic distractor diagnoses were replaced
with option-specific physical reasoning. No canonical question or answer data
was changed.
