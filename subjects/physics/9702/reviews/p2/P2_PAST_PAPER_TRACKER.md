## Status contract

- `NOT REVIEWED`: you have not completed the full review.
- `PASS`: you personally verified every required item in the PASS checklist and found no issue.
- `FAIL`: you found at least one issue. Record the exact defect in Notes.

A `SOURCE ONLY` question cannot be marked `PASS` because its official mark-scheme and enrichment packages are absent.

## PASS checklist

- Question text, parts, marks, dependencies and figures are complete.
- Official answer and mark scheme match the question.
- Topic, module, outcome, skill, pattern and knowledge references are correct.
- Difficulty is reasonable.
- Every hint is correct, question-specific, progressive and does not leak the answer too early.
- Every walkthrough is correct, complete, ordered and teaches the actual solution.
- Checking mode, deterministic checks and AI rubric cover all required marking points and valid alternatives.
- No copied, generic, contradictory or unrelated content remains.
