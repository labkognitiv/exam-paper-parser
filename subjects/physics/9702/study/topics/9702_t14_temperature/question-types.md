### Archetype: Definition, Law & Conservation Principle

**Example 1 (9702_m16_42_q03_a)** - Marks: 2
- **Question Prompt**: Define specific heat capacity.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: the (thermal) energy per unit mass to raise the temperature of a substance by one degree
- **Target Answer**: The thermal energy required per unit mass to raise the temperature of a substance by $1\text{ K}$ (or $1^{\circ}\text{C}$).

**Example 2 (9702_w17_42_q02_a)** - Marks: 2
- **Question Prompt**: State what is meant by $specific\ latent\ heat$.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: (thermal) energy per (unit) mass (to cause change of state) (energy required to cause/released in) change of state
- **Target Answer**: The thermal energy required per unit mass to cause a change of state, with no change in temperature.
