### Archetype: Definition, Law & Conservation Principle

**Example 1 (9702_m16_22_q04_a_i)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: By reference to the direction of propagation of energy, state what is meant by a $transverse$ wave.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Displacement of particles perpendicular to direction of energy propagation
- **Target Answer**: A transverse wave is one in which the displacement of the particles is perpendicular to the direction of propagation of energy.

**Example 2 (9702_m16_22_q04_a_ii)** - Marks: 2
- **Question Prompt**: State the principle of superposition.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: waves meet / overlap (at a point) (resultant) displacement is sum of the individual displacements
- **Target Answer**: When two waves meet at a point, the resultant displacement is the vector sum of the individual displacements of the two waves.
