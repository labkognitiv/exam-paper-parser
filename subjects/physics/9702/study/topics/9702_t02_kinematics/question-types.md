## Authentic Cambridge Past Paper Examples

### Archetype: Quantitative Calculation & Multi-Step Derivation

**Example 1 (9702_m16_12_q06)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 6 An object has an initial velocity u and an acceleration a. The object moves in a straight line through a displacement s and has final velocity v. The above quantities are related by the equation shown. v 2 = u 2 + 2as ...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: The equation $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$ is a SUVAT equation and is valid only for uniform acceleration, meaning constant magnitude and constant direction of $a$.
- **Target Answer**: Option C

**Example 2 (9702_s16_11_q08)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 8 Two cars X and Y are positioned as shown at time t = 0. They are travelling in the same direction. X is 50 m behind Y and has a constant velocity of 30 m s-1. Y has a constant velocity of 20 m s-1. 30 m s-1 X 20 m s-1 ...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: X gains on Y at $30-20 = 10 \text{m s}^{-1}$, so closing 50 m takes $50/10 = 5.0 \text{s}$.
- **Target Answer**: Option D

### Archetype: Graphical Sketching & Trend Interpretation

**Example 1 (9702_m16_12_q07)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 7 A car is travelling at constant velocity. Its brakes are then applied, causing uniform deceleration. Which graph shows the variation with distance s of the velocity v of the car? A B C D v v v v s s s s 0 0 0 0 0 0 0 0
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Horizontal $v$ then a curve that gets steeper as $s$ increases matches $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$ with negative $a$.
- **Target Answer**: Option A

**Example 2 (9702_s16_11_q06)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 6 A car accelerates uniformly from velocity u to velocity v in time t. velocity S v Q R u P W T N V U 00 t t 2 time On the graph, which area equals the distance travelled by the car in time t ? A NPTU + PQST B NPW V + VR...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: NPWV is $u \times t/2$ and VRSU is $v \times t/2$, so their sum is $(u+v)t/2$ which equals the area under PS from $0$ to $t$.
- **Target Answer**: Option B

### Archetype: Physical Mechanism & Field Explanation

**Example 1 (9702_m16_12_q08)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 8 A ball is thrown across a flat field. path of ball Which statement describes the motion of the ball, when the effects of air resistance are ignored? A The ball lands with the same velocity at which it is thrown. B The ...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: With no air resistance there is no horizontal force, so $a_x = 0$ throughout the flight, which is a constant value.
- **Target Answer**: Option B

**Example 2 (9702_s16_13_q08)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 8 A ball travels from point X to point Y around a semi-circular track of radius 1.0 m as shown. X ball north east west 1.0 m south Y What is the displacement of the ball from X to Y? A 2.0 m B 2.0 m due south C 3.1 m D 3...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Displacement is the straight line from X to Y, equal to the diameter $2 \times 1.0\text{ m} = 2.0\text{ m}$ directed due south.
- **Target Answer**: Option B

### Archetype: Definition, Law & Conservation Principle

**Example 1 (9702_s17_22_q04_a)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: State Newton’s first law of motion.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: a body/mass/object continues (at rest or) at constant/uniform velocity unless acted on by a resultant force
- **Target Answer**: a body/mass/object continues (at rest or) at constant/uniform velocity unless acted on by a resultant force

**Example 2 (9702_s24_23_q02_a)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: Define displacement from a point.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: distance (from the point) in a straight line in a given direction
- **Target Answer**: Displacement is the distance from the point in a straight line in a given direction.
