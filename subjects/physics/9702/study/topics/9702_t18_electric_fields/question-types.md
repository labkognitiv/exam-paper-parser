### Archetype: Quantitative Calculation & Multi-Step Derivation

**Example 1 (9702_m16_42_q09_b)** - Marks: 3
- **Question Prompt**: Calculate the magnitude of the electric field strength.  Explain your working.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: force due to electric field = force due to magnetic field or $Eq = Bqv$ $E = Bv$ ; $= 9.7 \times 10^{-2} \times 1.6 \times 10^{5}$ $= 1.6\ (1.55) \times 10^{4} \text{V m}^{-1}$
- **Target Answer**: For zero deflection $Eq = Bqv$, so $E = Bv = 9.7 \times 10^{-2} \times 1.6 \times 10^{5} = 1.6 \times 10^{4} \text{ V m}^{-1}$.
