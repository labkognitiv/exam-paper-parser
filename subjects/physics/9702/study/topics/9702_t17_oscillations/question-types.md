### Archetype: Definition, Law & Conservation Principle

**Example 1 (9702_m18_42_q04_a)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: Explain what is meant by the natural frequency of vibration of a system.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: when there is no (resultant external) resistive force and when there is no driving force / external force
- **Target Answer**: The natural frequency is the frequency of free oscillation of a system with no driving force and no (resultant) resistive damping force.

**Example 2 (9702_w18_42_q05_a)** - Marks: 2
- **Question Prompt**: In radio communication, the radio wave is usually modulated.  State what is meant by amplitude modulation (AM).
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: amplitude of carrier (wave) varies variation in synchrony with displacement of information signal
- **Target Answer**: The amplitude of the carrier wave is varied in synchrony with the displacement of the information signal.
