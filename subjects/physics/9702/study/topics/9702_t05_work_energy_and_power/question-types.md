### Archetype: Quantitative Calculation & Multi-Step Derivation

**Example 1 (9702_m16_12_q15)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 15 The force diagram shows an aircraft accelerating. At the instant shown, the velocity of the aircraft is 40 m s–1. velocity 40 m s–1 600 kN lift 500 kN engine thrust 200 kN air resistance 600 kN weight At which rate is...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Net forward force $300 \text{ kN}$ times $40 \text{ m s}^{-1}$ gives $12 \times 10^6 \text{ W} = 12 \text{ MW}$ increase in kinetic energy.
- **Target Answer**: Option C

**Example 2 (9702_m16_12_q17)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 17 The pump of a water pumping system uses 2.0 kW of electrical power when raising water. The pumping system lifts 16 kg of water per second through a vertical height of 7.0 m. What is the efficiency of the pumping syste...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Useful output power $mgh/t$ is about $1100 \text{ W}$, so efficiency is about $1100/2000 = 0.55 = 55\%$.
- **Target Answer**: Option D

### Archetype: Definition, Law & Conservation Principle

**Example 1 (9702_w17_13_q15)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 15 A ball is thrown vertically upwards. Air resistance is negligible. Which statement is correct? A By the principle of conservation of energy, the total energy of the ball is constant throughout its motion. B By the pri...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: With air resistance negligible, kinetic energy and gravitational potential energy only interconvert with no energy dissipated, so total $E_k + E_p$ is constant.
- **Target Answer**: Option A

**Example 2 (9702_m19_12_q15)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 15 Which statement best represents the principle of conservation of energy? A Energy cannot be used faster than it is created. B The supply of energy is limited, so energy must be conserved. C The total energy in a close...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Energy cannot be created or destroyed, so with no transfer in or out of a closed system the sum of all stores stays constant.
- **Target Answer**: Option C

### Archetype: Experimental Uncertainty & Measurement Analysis

**Example 1 (9702_m16_12_q16)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 16 A man is running in a straight line. What is an approximate value of his kinetic energy? A 10 J B 100 J C 1000 J D 10 000 J
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Using $m \approx 70\text{ kg}$ and $v \approx 5\text{ m s}^{-1}$ gives $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 \approx 900\text{ J}$, closest to $1000\text{ J}$.
- **Target Answer**: Option C

**Example 2 (9702_s19_13_q04)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 4 What is the approximate kinetic energy of an Olympic athlete when running at maximum speed during a 100 m race? A 400 J B 4000 J C 40 000 J D 400 000 J
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: With $m \approx 80\text{ kg}$ and $v \approx 10\text{ m s}^{-1}$, $\frac{1}{2}mv^2 \approx 4000\text{ J}$.
- **Target Answer**: Option B
