### Archetype: Definition, Law & Conservation Principle

**Example 1 (9702_m16_42_q07_a)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: Define *capacitance*.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: (capacitance =) charge / potential (difference)
- **Target Answer**: Capacitance is the charge stored per unit potential difference across the capacitor, $C = Q/V$.

**Example 2 (9702_s17_42_q07_b)** - Marks: 2
- **Question Prompt**: Define the capacitance of the capacitor.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: charge / potential difference reference to charge on one plate
- **Target Answer**: Capacitance is the charge stored on one plate of the capacitor per unit potential difference between the plates, $C = Q/V$.
