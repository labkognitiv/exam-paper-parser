## Authentic Cambridge Past Paper Examples

### Archetype: Quantitative Calculation & Multi-Step Derivation

**Example 1 (9702_m16_12_q28)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 28 The electric field strength at a certain distance from an isolated alpha particle is 3.0 × 107 N C–1. What is the force on an electron when at that distance from the alpha particle? A 4.8 × 10–12 N B 9.6 × 10–12 N C 3...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Magnitude $F = EQ = 3.0 \times 10^7 \times 1.6 \times 10^{-19} = 4.8 \times 10^{-12}$ $\text{N}$.
- **Target Answer**: Option A

**Example 2 (9702_m16_12_q29)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 29 Two large parallel plates X and Y are placed a distance of 5.0 mm apart and connected to the terminals of a 200 V d.c. supply, as shown. X Y P 5.0 mm 200 V A small oil drop at P carries one excess electron. What is th...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Field $E = V/d = 4.0 \times 10^4 \text{ V m}^{-1}$ gives $F = qE = 1.60 \times 10^{-19} \times 4.0 \times 10^4 = 6.4 \times 10^{-15} \text{ N}$.
- **Target Answer**: Option A

### Archetype: Graphical Sketching & Trend Interpretation

**Example 1 (9702_s16_11_q32)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 32 A 100 Ω resistor conducts a current with changing direction and magnitude, as shown. 2 current / A 0 1 0 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 time / ms –1 What is the mean power dissipated in the resistor? A 100 W B 150 W C 250 W D 400 W
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Mean $I^2 = 2.5 \text{ A}^2$, so mean power $= 2.5 \times 100 = 250 \text{ W}$.
- **Target Answer**: Option C

**Example 2 (9702_s16_11_q33)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 33 Which graph shows the I–V characteristic of a filament lamp? A B C D I I I I V V V V 00 00 00 00
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Shows gradient decreasing with $V$, so resistance increases as the filament heats up, which matches a filament lamp.
- **Target Answer**: Option D

### Archetype: Physical Mechanism & Field Explanation

**Example 1 (9702_m16_12_q27)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 27 The diagram shows an electron in a uniform electric field. In which direction will the field accelerate the electron? electron A electric D B field C
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Direction D is opposite to the field arrows. Since the electron charge $q$ is negative, $F = qE$ is opposite to $E$.
- **Target Answer**: Option D

**Example 2 (9702_s16_11_q28)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 28 A charged particle is moving in a uniform electric field. For the motion of the particle due to the field, which quantity has a constant non-zero value? A acceleration B displacement C rate of change of acceleration D...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Uniform field gives constant force $F = qE$ and so constant $a = F/m$, which is non-zero because $q$ and $E$ are non-zero.
- **Target Answer**: Option A

### Archetype: Definition, Law & Conservation Principle

**Example 1 (9702_s16_12_q34)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 34 Which equation is used to define resistance? A energy = (current)2 × resistance × time B potential difference = current × resistance C power = (current)2 × resistance D resistivity = resistance × area ÷ length
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Resistance is defined by the ratio of potential difference across a component to current through it, $R = V/I$, so $V = IR$.
- **Target Answer**: Option B

**Example 2 (9702_m16_22_q05_a_i)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: State what is meant by an electric current.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: movement/flow of charge carriers
- **Target Answer**: Electric current is the rate of flow of charge, or the movement/flow of charge carriers.

### Archetype: Diagrammatic Vector & Circuit Construction

**Example 1 (9702_s16_11_q29)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 29 Which diagram could represent the electric field lines between two oppositely charged conducting surfaces? B A D C
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: All lines leave the flat face horizontally and meet the concave surface along its normal, so they are perpendicular to both conductors.
- **Target Answer**: Option D

**Example 2 (9702_s16_12_q29)** - Marks: 1

- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Lines go from positive to negative, leave the sphere radially and meet the negative plate at right angles, with correct curved shape.
- **Target Answer**: Option B

### Archetype: Experimental Uncertainty & Measurement Analysis

**Example 1 (9702_m16_12_q32)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 32 Which measurements are taken in order to calculate the resistivity of the metal of a piece of wire? A p.d., current, area, length B p.d., current, diameter, length C resistance, area, length D resistance, length, radi...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: p.d. and current give $R$, and measured diameter gives $A$, so $\rho = RA/L$ can be found from direct measurements.
- **Target Answer**: Option B

**Example 2 (9702_w16_11_q35)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: 35 What is a typical value for the order of magnitude of the resistivity of copper? A 10–13 Ω m B 10–8 Ω m C 10–3 Ω m D 102 Ω m
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Copper is an excellent conductor with resistivity about $1.7 \times 10^{-8}$ $\Omega \text{m}$, so order $10^{-8}$ $\Omega \text{m}$.
- **Target Answer**: Option B
