### Archetype: Quantitative Calculation & Multi-Step Derivation

**Example 1 (9702_m16_42_q01_b)** - Marks: 2
- **Question Prompt**: A satellite of mass $m$ has a circular orbit of radius $r$ about a planet of mass $M$. It may be assumed that the planet and the satellite are uniform spheres that are isolated in space.  Show that the linear speed $v$ o...
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: gravitational force provides / is the centripetal force GMm/r^2 = mv^2/r or GMm/r^2 = mrω^2 and v = rω and algebra leading to v = (GM/r)^{1/2}
- **Target Answer**: Gravitational force provides the centripetal force: $GMm/r^2 = mv^2/r$, giving $v^2 = GM/r$ so $v = \sqrt{GM/r}$.

**Example 2 (9702_m16_42_q01_c_ii)** - Marks: 2
- **Question Prompt**: The planet spins about its own axis with angular speed $1.7 \times 10^{-4} \text{rad s}^{-1}$. Moon A is always above the same point on the planet's surface.  Determine the orbital period $T_{B}$ of moon B.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: T = 2π / 1.7 × 10^{-4} = 3.70 × 10^4 s T_B = 3.70 × 10^4 / 4.54 × 10^{-4} = 8.1 × 10^7 s
- **Target Answer**: $T_A = 2\pi/\omega = 3.70 \times 10^4 \text{ s}$, so $T_B = T_A / 4.54 \times 10^{-4} = 8.1 \times 10^7 \text{ s}$.
