### Archetype: Quantitative Calculation & Multi-Step Derivation

**Example 1 (9702_w16_41_q02_b_i)** - Marks: 2
- **Question Prompt**: The increase in internal energy $\Delta U$ of a system may be represented by the expression $$\Delta U = q + w.$$
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: 1. heating/thermal energy supplied 2. work done on/to system
- **Target Answer**: 1. $+q$ is thermal energy (heating) supplied to the system. 2. $+w$ is work done on the system.

**Example 2 (9702_w16_41_q02_b_ii)** - Marks: 3
- **Question Prompt**: State, for the gas in (a), the value of  1. $\Delta U$,  2. $+q$,  3. $+w$.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: 1. 240 J 2. same value as given in 1. (= 240 J) and zero given for 3. 3. zero
- **Target Answer**: $\Delta U = 240\text{ J}$, $+q = 240\text{ J}$, $+w = 0\text{ J}$
