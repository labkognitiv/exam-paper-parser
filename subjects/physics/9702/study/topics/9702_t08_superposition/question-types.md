### Archetype: Physical Mechanism & Field Explanation

**Example 1 (9702_m16_12_q23)** - Marks: 1

- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: Diffraction is the spreading of waves past an edge or through a gap, so sound can bend round a corner into the shadow region.
- **Target Answer**: Option A

**Example 2 (9702_m16_12_q24)** - Marks: 1

- **Target Answer**: Option B

### Archetype: Definition, Law & Conservation Principle

**Example 1 (9702_s18_21_q05_a_i)** - Marks: 1
- **Question Prompt**: When monochromatic light is incident normally on a diffraction grating, the emergent light waves have been diffracted and are coherent.  Explain what is meant by
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: waves spread at (each) slit/gap
- **Target Answer**: Waves spread out as they pass through each slit of the grating.

**Example 2 (9702_w18_21_q04_a)** - Marks: 2
- **Question Prompt**: State the principle of superposition.
- **Mark Scheme Criteria**: when (two or more) waves meet (at a point) (resultant) displacement is the sum of the individual displacements
- **Target Answer**: When two or more waves meet at a point, the resultant displacement is the vector sum of the individual displacements of each wave.
