## Shared visual language

- Pure white background, black/dark-grey thin-to-medium strokes, generous space,
  simple sans-serif labels, short unambiguous leaders, minimal shading/colour.
- No title, answer, watermark, decorative caption, invented scale, unsupported
  label, perspective distortion, or irrelevant object.
- All arrows have one precise meaning and do not cross unnecessarily.
- Give axes quantities, units, justified scales, legible ticks, and correctly
  placed data/curves. Preserve negative regions, tangent geometry, and shaded
  areas when they carry meaning.

## Physics conventions

- **Circuits:** prescribed component symbols, junction dots, unambiguous
  crossings, continuous wires, correct meter connections, switch state, source
  polarity, and current direction. Topology is immutable under responsive layout.
- **Ray diagrams:** arrowed propagation, normal at interface, angles from the
  normal, correct optical axis/foci, correct reflection/refraction, and dashed
  virtual extensions where needed.
- **Force diagrams:** isolate the body; use force arrows with correct origin,
  direction, line of action and label. Use scaled lengths only when justified.
- **Apparatus:** possible connections and supports, relevant controls, readable
  instrument scales/resolution, clear reference points, and explicit viewpoint.
- **Fields/waves/vectors:** correct arrow direction, line/spacing convention,
  phase, nodes, components and scale. State `not to scale` when appropriate.

## Provenance and original production

An official copied/cropped figure retains original pixels. Record canonical
package path, paper code, component, year/session/variant, question/figure number,
source dimensions and SHA-256, crop rectangle, output dimensions and SHA-256,
and visible official attribution. Reject crops that remove a legend, scale, axis,
label, arrow, or contextual object needed for interpretation.

Build answer-critical originals deterministically. ImageGen may supply only a
qualitative context scene in `scientific-educational` mode on pure white. Compare
every visible object against the question and reject scientific ambiguity,
answer leakage, illegible content, or invented measurements.
